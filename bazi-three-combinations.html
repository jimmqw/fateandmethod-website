Ba Zi — The Four Pillars

The Three Combinations
Dragon, Tiger & Phoenix

Among the most potent formations in Ba Zi are the Three Harmonies — three pillars that combine when heaven, earth, and human energies align. Here is how they work, and what they mean for your chart.

Home / Ba Zi / Three Combinations

In Ba Zi, the Three Combinations (三合, San He) represent one of the most powerful structural formations you can find in a chart. Unlike the straightforward push-pull of the Day Master against Hourly Branches, the Three Combinations describe a deeper harmony — a resonance between three pillars that, when present, can transform the entire trajectory of a life. They are not common. But when they appear, they tend to matter in a way that few other chart formations do.

The basic principle is elemental: Water generates Wood, Wood generates Fire, Fire generates Earth (Earth produces Metal), Metal generates Water. The Three Combinations are built from three adjacent branches in a specific generative sequence — and when all three appear in a Ba Zi chart, they create a resonance that amplifies everything in their vicinity.

id="the-three-combinations-an-overview"

There are four sets of Three Combinations, each aligned with one of the four directional trigrams. Each set combines a Stem, a Branch from each of the three compatible zodiac signs, and the branch of the controlling animal in that sequence.

🌲

Wood Dragon, Tiger, Dragon

Jia Yin · Yin Mao · Chen Dragon

The Wood Combination forms when Jia (甲, Yang Wood) sits in the Year Pillar, combined with the branches of the Dragon (辰, Chen) and Tiger (寅, Yin). It activates when Wood energy is dominant and the chart's Day Master has sufficient strength to carry it.

🔥

Fire Phoenix, Snake, Tiger

Bing Wu · Si Snake · Yin Tiger

The Fire Combination forms when Bing (丙, Yang Fire) appears in the Year Pillar, combined with Snake (巳, Si) and Tiger (寅, Yin). It is one of the most dramatic combinations — associated with high visibility, public recognition, and powerful drive.

⚪

Metal Phoenix, Rooster, Monkey

Geng Yin · You Rooster · Shen Monkey

The Metal Combination forms when Geng (庚, Yang Metal) is in the Year Pillar, combined with Rooster (酉, You) and Monkey (申, Shen). It represents authority, precision, and the capacity to organize and systematize complex problems.

💧

Water Phoenix, Rat, Dragon

Ren Zi · Zi Rat · Chen Dragon

The Water Combination forms when Ren (壬, Yang Water) appears in the Year Pillar, combined with Rat (子, Zi) and Dragon (辰, Chen). Water Combination is the most fluid and adaptable of the four — associated with communication, movement, and intelligence that flows around obstacles rather than through them directly.

Note on Earth Combination: There is technically a fifth set, the Earth Combination (土, Earth) combining Dragon, Dog, and Monkey — but it functions differently and is often considered in a separate category (四库, the Four Repositories). Many practitioners treat it distinctly from the core Three Combinations, and it requires different interpretive rules.

id="how-the-three-combinations-form-in-a-chart"

A Three Combination is formed when any two of the three elements appear in a chart — the Year Pillar, Month Pillar, or Hourly Branch — and the third is present in at least one supporting position. The full combination, when all three elements are present, creates the maximum resonance effect. Partial combinations — two of the three elements — still carry significance, though weaker.

Three Levels of Combination Strength
Activation · Completeness · Strength

Full Three Combination

All three branches appear across the four pillars. Year, Month, and Hourly Branch each carry one of the required branches. This creates the maximum harmonizing effect — the native tends to experience life as unusually coherent and purposeful, with a clear direction that others recognize.

Two-Pillar Partial

Two of the three branches appear in the same pillar pair (e.g., Year and Month together, or Year and Hour). This creates a partial resonance — the combination energy is present but not fully activated. The native may feel the pull toward the combination's themes without fully embodying them.

Hidden Combination (藏干)

When the combination elements are present in the hidden stems rather than the visible ones, the combination is said to be hidden. It still influences the chart but in a less obvious, more internalized way. The native may experience the combination's themes as an inner conviction rather than an outer identity.

id="what-each-combination-reveals-about-life-path"

Wood Combination (三合木局)

The Wood Combination, when activated, produces individuals of exceptional growth energy — literally: Wood is the element of expansion, upward movement, and germination. People with a strong Wood Combination tend to be energetic, idealistic, and oriented toward building things that did not previously exist. They see possibilities where others see problems.

The challenge of Wood Combination is that Wood can become rigid when it lacks flexibility — it can grow in the wrong direction if not properly directed. Charts with strong Wood Combination need Water (the generating element) to remain fluid and adaptive. Without it, Wood risks becoming stubborn, unable to bend, and prone to breaking under pressure.

Fire Combination (三合火局)

Fire Combination is the most dramatic of the four. Fire is light, visibility, and transformative heat — people with a strong Fire Combination tend to draw attention simply by being present. They are often found in careers that require public performance, charisma, or the ability to inspire and move groups of people.

The risk of Fire Combination is overheating: too much Fire without sufficient Water to balance creates volatility, impatience, and a tendency to burn through relationships and resources without sufficient care for sustainability. The Fire Combination native needs to learn the difference between heat that illuminates and heat that destroys.

Metal Combination (三合金局)

Metal Combination produces precision, authority, and organizational capability. Metal is the element of tools, instruments, and structured systems — individuals with this combination tend to excel at bringing order to complex situations. They are often drawn to positions of management, technical expertise, or systems design.

The shadow of Metal Combination is rigidity: Metal that is not properly tempered by Fire can become brittle, overly judgmental, and unwilling to adapt when circumstances change. The Metal Combination native benefits from learning to see nuance where their natural tendency is to see categories.

Water Combination (三合水局)

Water Combination is associated with adaptability, communication, and intelligence that flows around rather than through obstacles. Water is the element of wisdom in its classical Chinese sense — not book knowledge, but the kind of understanding that comes from observing patterns and responding to them appropriately.

The risk of Water Combination is diffusion: Water that spreads too thin loses its depth and power. The Water Combination native needs structure and focus to prevent their natural adaptability from becoming a lack of direction. When well-balanced, Water Combination individuals are often described by others as unusually insightful and perceptive.

id="how-to-detect-a-three-combination-in-your-chart"

Identifying a Three Combination in a Ba Zi chart requires checking the hidden stems (藏干) in each pillar as well as the visible ones, as the combination can be present in either form. Here is the practical method:

Step-by-Step Detection
Process · Method · Checklist

Step 1 — Identify the dominant element. Look at the Day Master and the overall elemental balance of the chart. Which element is the Day Master, and which elements appear most strongly across the four pillars?

Step 2 — Cross-reference the generating sequence. Check whether the branches of the three animals in any generating sequence appear anywhere in the chart — Year, Month, Day, or Hour pillars, as well as the hidden stems of each pillar. The generating sequences are: Dragon–Tiger–Dragon (Wood), Snake–Tiger–Phoenix (Fire), Monkey–Rooster–Phoenix (Metal), Rat–Dragon–Phoenix (Water).

Step 3 — Check the Year Pillar stem. The stem of the Year Pillar must align with the generating element of the combination for it to be considered a true Three Combination. A chart with two matching branches but the wrong Year Stem is a partial combination, not a full one.

Wood Combination Key Words

Growth, expansion, new ventures, idealism, leadership, building from nothing, entrepreneurship, learning, adaptability in growth.

Fire Combination Key Words

Visibility, recognition, charisma, influence, passion, public role, inspiration, transformation, leadership through example.

Metal Combination Key Words

Precision, authority, systems, organization, justice, clarity, management, structure, technical expertise.

Water Combination Key Words

Adaptability, intelligence, communication, flow, wisdom, insight, media, travel, diplomacy, flexible strategy.

Common Misinterpretation: A common beginner error is to see two animals from the same generating sequence and conclude a Three Combination is present. The Year Pillar's stem must match the combination element for the full formation to be active. Partial combinations (without the correct stem) still carry interpretive weight but do not produce the full resonance effect that defines a true Three Combination.

id="the-three-combinations-and-life-domains"

Each combination tends to express most strongly in specific life domains:

🌲 Wood

Career growth, learning, creative ventures, family legacy, early-life development, physical health

🔥 Fire

Public reputation, fame, leadership, career peaks, social influence, romantic partnerships

⚪ Metal

Wealth accumulation, structured careers, later-life authority, inheritance, accumulated resources

This domain association follows the natural arc of the generating cycle: Wood governs growth and establishment in early life, Fire governs the peak years of visibility and influence, and Metal governs the accumulation and consolidation of what was built. Water, being the most fluid, tends to express across all domains depending on what the chart most needs.

id="when-the-three-combinations-clash-with-other-formations"

A Three Combination does not exist in isolation — it interacts with the rest of the chart's formations. The most important interactions to check:

Combination vs. Clash (冲): If a pillar in the Three Combination is being challenged by a clashing pillar from another pillar pair, the resonance effect may be suppressed or distorted. The combination still exists, but the native may experience the combination's qualities through struggle rather than flow.

Combination vs. Punitive Stem (刑): Certain stem-branch relationships create internal friction within the combination itself. When the branches in a Three Combination are in aPunishment relationship with each other, the combination may activate incompletely, creating one-sided or conflicted expression of the combination's themes.

Combination vs. Empty (空亡): When one of the three required branches falls into an Empty Pillar (空亡) — where the heavenly stems do not support any of the twelve branches — the combination may be present but inactive. The native has the potential, but circumstances have not yet aligned to activate it.
